Arrival

Two locations, self-travel

The exhibition starts at 3:00 PM. We recommend finishing by 5:30 PM and heading to the dinner venue. Dinner begins at 6:30 PM, and everyone must be seated by 6:30 PM. Please arrive early, thank you!

Best Dressed

Results announced at 8:30 PM

Voting closes at 8:30 PM. We will crown Top Female and Top Male, followed by two overall Best Dressed winners. Small prizes will be awarded — dress to impress ✨

Voting Rules

One vote per person

Please enter your name exactly as shown in the candidate list. One person, one vote. And yes, you may vote for yourself. Voting is not anonymous.
